What to expect

When you reach out, tell us a little about your business — what you do, roughly how large you are, and what you need help with. That lets us respond usefully rather than with generic questions. We aim to reply to every enquiry within one business day. If your matter is time-sensitive (a filing deadline, an IRD notice), say so, and we will prioritise it.

Who we work with

We serve small and growing businesses across Nepal, with particular experience in the travel & tourism, manufacturing, trading, energy and education sectors. Most of our work is based in and around Kathmandu, but we support clients throughout the country, and much of our compliance and bookkeeping work is handled remotely with secure, cloud-based tools. New businesses, established companies and organisations preparing for an external audit are all welcome to get in touch.

A note on privacy

Anything you share with us when you make an enquiry is used only to respond to you and to provide the service you request. We never sell or trade your information. You can read the details on our Privacy Policy page.
