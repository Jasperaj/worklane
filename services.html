Expertise

What we do for you.

Financial advisory · Nepal Compliance · Accounting · Advisory · Assurance

Worklane offers four connected lines of work. Most clients start with one — usually recurring compliance or bookkeeping — and grow into others as they come to rely on us. Everything below can be delivered as an ongoing retainer or as a focused, one-off engagement, and wherever it helps we bring modern accounting software and automation into the mix to make the work faster and more accurate.

(01)

Management Services & Virtual CFO

This is the strategic layer of what we do — the advice that helps you plan, structure and steer the business rather than simply record what has already happened. It suits founders who need the judgement of a finance chief without the cost of a full-time hire.

  • Business registration & structuring — company formation, PAN/VAT registration and the regulatory setup a new or restructuring business needs to start on solid ground.
  • Financial due diligence — pre-investment analysis for acquisitions, partnerships and fundraising, so you understand exactly what you are buying into.
  • MIS reporting & dashboards — management information tailored to how you actually make decisions, not generic reports that sit unread.
  • Budgeting & forecasting — annual budgets, scenario models and variance analysis that turn ambition into a plan you can track.
  • Internal controls review — identifying risk and recommending practical process improvements before problems occur.
  • Virtual CFO services — part-time financial leadership for growing businesses, giving you senior oversight of cash, margins and strategy month to month.
(02)

Tax, Customs & VAT Compliance

Tax in Nepal moves quickly and the penalties for getting it wrong are real. We handle the full compliance cycle with the Inland Revenue Department and keep you on the right side of every deadline — while making sure you never pay more than the law requires.

  • Corporate tax planning & filing — annual income-tax returns, advance-tax computation and end-to-end IRD compliance.
  • VAT registration & periodic returns — monthly and quarterly VAT filing with proper purchase/sales reconciliation.
  • TDS management — withholding-tax computation, timely deposit and deduction tracking across your suppliers and payroll.
  • Customs duty advisory — import/export compliance and duty-classification guidance for trading and manufacturing businesses.
  • Tax dispute resolution — representing you in IRD inquiries, assessments and reassessments.
  • Advance tax structuring — reducing your tax burden through lawful, well-documented planning rather than guesswork.
(03)

Accounting & Bookkeeping

Reliable books are the foundation everything else rests on. We keep your day-to-day accounting accurate and current, so that when it is time to file, report or make a decision, the numbers are already trustworthy.

  • Full-cycle bookkeeping — accounts payable and receivable, bank reconciliation and clean, up-to-date ledgers.
  • Financial statement preparation — balance sheets, profit-and-loss statements and cash-flow statements you can rely on.
  • Payroll processing — staff salary computation, PF/CIT contributions and correct tax deductions, run on time.
  • Accounting software setup & training — implementation and training on Tally, Busy, Tigg and cloud-based platforms.
  • AI-assisted finance automation — workflow tools that cut manual effort and reduce reporting errors, freeing your team for higher-value work.
(04)

Assurance & Internal Review

We are not a statutory audit firm, and we do not sign audit opinions. What we do is help you get your house in order — strengthening controls, verifying what you own, and making sure you are ready before an external auditor or regulator ever arrives.

  • Internal audit — process-level reviews that strengthen operational controls and efficiency.
  • Physical assets verification — stock counts, fixed-asset tagging and reconciliation to your records.
  • Cash & bank reconciliation review — spot checks and investigation of variances before they compound.
  • Pre-audit preparation — organising records and schedules so your external audit runs smoothly and without surprises.
  • Compliance health check — a regulatory-readiness review across IRD, PAN/VAT and company filings.
