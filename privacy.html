Privacy Policy

Worklane Financial Advisory Last updated: 27 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Worklane Financial Advisory ("Worklane", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, discloses and protects information when you visit worklane.com.np (the "Website"). Worklane is a financial advisory practice based in Hadigaun, Kathmandu, Nepal. We take your privacy seriously and are committed to handling any information you share with care, transparency and in line with applicable law.

By using the Website, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please discontinue use of the Website. This policy applies only to worklane.com.np and does not cover any third-party websites we may link to.

Information we collect

We aim to collect as little personal information as possible. The information we may collect falls into two broad categories:

Information you give us voluntarily

When you contact us — for example by emailing upaj.worklane@gmail.com, calling us, or otherwise reaching out for a consultation or enquiry — you may share your name, email address, phone number, business details and the content of your message. We use this information solely to respond to you and to provide the services you request. Providing this information is entirely voluntary, but without it we may be unable to answer your enquiry.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, when you visit worklane.com.np our hosting provider and third-party services may automatically record standard technical information such as your device type, browser type and version, operating system, approximate location (derived from IP address), the pages you view, the date and time of your visit, and the site that referred you. This information is aggregated and statistical in nature and is used to understand how the Website is used and to keep it secure and functioning correctly.

Cookies and similar technologies

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device by a website you visit. Cookies are widely used to make websites work, to make them more efficient, and to provide reporting information to site owners and advertisers.

The Website itself uses minimal cookies. However, third-party services we use — in particular Google AdSense (see below) — may set their own cookies. You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or to alert you when a cookie is being sent. Please note that if you disable cookies, some parts of the Website or third-party features may not function as intended.

Advertising and Google AdSense

We may use Google AdSense, an advertising service provided by Google LLC, to display advertisements on the Website. Advertising helps us keep our content free to read. In connection with serving ads, the following applies:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this Website and other websites on the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices.

Third-party ad networks may collect information such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you visit and the time of your visit in order to measure and improve the ads they serve. We do not control these third-party cookies and their behaviour is governed by the providers' own privacy policies. For more detail on how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see Google's Privacy & Terms.

Analytics

We may use privacy-conscious analytics tools to understand aggregate traffic patterns — for example, which articles are most read — so that we can improve our content. Any analytics data is used in aggregate and is not used to identify individual visitors.

How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • respond to your enquiries and provide the advisory services you request;
  • operate, maintain and improve the Website and its content;
  • understand how visitors use the Website so we can make it more useful;
  • display relevant advertising through Google AdSense; and
  • comply with our legal and regulatory obligations.

Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We may share information only with:

  • Service providers that help us operate the Website (such as hosting and advertising providers), who are permitted to use the information only to perform services on our behalf;
  • Professional and regulatory bodies where disclosure is required to provide a service you have engaged us for; and
  • Authorities where we are legally required to disclose information, or to protect our rights, safety or property.

Data retention and security

We keep enquiry information only for as long as necessary to respond to you and to meet any legal or record-keeping requirements, after which it is deleted or anonymised. We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information we hold against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights and choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information you have shared with us by emailing upaj.worklane@gmail.com. You can also control cookies through your browser and opt out of personalised advertising using the links in the Advertising section above. You are never required to provide personal information to read the content on this Website.

Children's privacy

The Website is intended for a general, professional audience and is not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can remove it.

Third-party links

Our content, including articles in the Worklane Journal, may link to external websites such as the Inland Revenue Department (IRD) portal or other reference sources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites. We encourage you to read the privacy policy of every site you visit.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology or legal requirements. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Your continued use of the Website after any change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
