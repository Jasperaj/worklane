About Worklane

Supporting businesses like yours.

Established 2020 · Kathmandu, Nepal Registered financial advisory practice

Worklane Financial Advisory is a Kathmandu-based practice built on a simple belief: every rupee has a story, and a business deserves to understand its own numbers clearly enough to act on them. Since 2020 we have worked alongside owners, founders and finance teams across Nepal — helping them stay compliant, make better decisions, and spend less time fighting spreadsheets and more time running their business.

We are not a large, impersonal firm. We are a focused advisory practice led by a chartered accountant, and that means the person who understands your accounts is the same person who picks up the phone. Our engagements are deliberately hands-on, and we bring a rare combination of deep accounting discipline and technology-forward thinking to every one of them.

What we do

Worklane provides end-to-end financial advisory for small and growing businesses in Nepal. That spans the everyday discipline of bookkeeping and payroll; the recurring obligations of VAT, TDS and income-tax compliance with the Inland Revenue Department; the strategic work of budgeting, forecasting and virtual-CFO support; and the internal-review work that keeps a company's controls honest. Wherever it makes sense, we layer modern accounting software and AI-assisted automation on top, so that reporting is faster, cleaner and less error-prone than manual processes allow.

You can read the full breakdown on our Services page, but the short version is this: if it touches your books, your tax position or your financial controls, it is something we can help you carry.

How we work

Good advice starts with understanding the business, not just the ledger. Every engagement begins with us learning how your company actually operates — how revenue comes in, where costs sit, what keeps you up at night — before we recommend anything. From there we build a working relationship rather than a one-off transaction: recurring compliance handled quietly in the background, and strategic input available when a decision calls for it.

We prize accuracy and plain speaking in equal measure. Numbers should be right, and they should be explained in language a business owner can use. We would rather tell you something inconvenient early than let it become a problem at filing time. And because compliance in Nepal changes often, we keep our own knowledge current — which is part of why we publish the Worklane Journal, our ongoing writing on tax, VAT, IRD procedure and financial strategy.

Sectors we serve

Over the past five years we have developed particular familiarity with the compliance realities and cost structures of a handful of industries:

Travel & Tourism Manufacturing Trading Energy Education

Each of these carries its own quirks — from the VAT and turnover-tax treatment of air-ticketing in travel, to inventory and fixed-asset discipline in manufacturing and trading, to the sector-specific reporting expected in energy and education. That accumulated, sector-aware experience is what lets us move quickly and avoid the generic advice that so often misses the point.
